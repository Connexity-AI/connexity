# Decisions

The decisions made while building Connexity, in order, and the questions still open.
Each pull request's own decisions are in its plan in [`plans/`](../plans/README.md);
the order of work is Dmytro's feature roadmap, kept outside the repo.

How a statement becomes a decision is in
[`development-lifecycle.md`](./development-lifecycle.md), section 4: only Dmytro's own
words make something Decided.

Older rows name files, phases and slice numbers from the time they were written, among
them `REBUILD.md`, the working plan this log was part of until 2026-11-20. They are
kept as written.

# 404 page and favicon

- **Owner:** Dmytro
- **Slice:** Demo milestone, roadmap feature "404 page" (10.165).
- **Design:** `Connexity 404.dc.html`, from the design review delivered 2026-11-18 (added
  to `designs/screens/` in this PR). The same review swaps the placeholder circles for
  the real logo in `Connexity MCP Docs`, `Connexity Sidebar`, `Connexity Sign in` and
  `Connexity Issue`; those four files and the logo asset are refreshed too. The app
  already shows the real logo, so they change nothing in the build.
- **MCP:** None needed: a page nobody reaches through the assistant, and no tool changes.

## Goal

A wrong address in the app lands on a calm page in Connexity's own voice, a failed call
transfer, with one way back to Work, instead of today's default page with a bird icon
and "Return to Home". And the browser tab shows the Connexity logo instead of today's
filled orange square.

## Decisions

- **Accept, build** (Dmytro, 2026-11-18).
- **The favicon becomes the real logo, in this task** (Dmytro, 2026-11-18: "as part of
  this task can we put a normal favicon cause current one is just a filled orange
  square").
- **The 404 as a failed call transfer** (Dmytro, 2026-11-18, chosen from four angles):
  the Connexity logo top left, a large 404, "Page not found", one sentence ("We tried to
  transfer you, but the line dropped. This page doesn't exist."), a small transfer
  diagram from "You" to a dashed "This page" whose line breaks halfway, and one button,
  "Back to Work". No sidebar. Calm motion only.

## Changes

- **`app/not-found.tsx`** rebuilt as the design, from the design system's tokens:
  - the logo (the existing `Brand`) top left, linking to Work;
  - "404" large (128px, 88px on a narrow screen), "Page not found", the sentence;
  - the diagram: a "You" box, a dashed line moving slowly towards a red cross, a faded
    dashed line, and a dashed "This page" box; hidden from screen readers;
  - "Back to Work" (`UrlGenerator.work()`); signed out, Work sends you to sign in as
    usual.
- **Motion:** only the dashes before the cross move, and not at all when the system asks
  for reduced motion.
- **Dark and light** from the tokens.
- **The favicon:** the orange three-loop mark (the design review's
  `assets/connexity-logo.png`, transparent) at 64px for the tab and 32px as a fallback,
  and a 180px Apple touch icon on an opaque background, since iOS fills transparency
  with black. Same paths in `constants/metadata.ts`, so nothing else changes. The logo
  is fixed (Dmytro, 2026-11-09); no new mark.

## Outcome

### Deviations from the plan

- **New icon file names** (`logo-64.png`, `logo-32.png`, `apple-icon.png`) instead of
  the same paths: browsers cache favicons hard, and a new name makes them fetch the
  logo. The old `favicon.png` is gone; nothing else used it.
- **The Apple icon** puts the loops on the dark surface (`#121314`), generated from the
  design review's logo with `sharp`, which Next already installs.
- **The moving dashes' keyframes** are a small `<style>` inside the 404 page, not in the
  shared stylesheet; nothing else uses them.

### Check results

`make check`: every step ok (plan, local database, backend lint and format, pyright,
backend tests, MCP server tests, frontend lint, frontend types, generated client).
The frontend build passes and lists `/_not-found`.

**`/code-review` (high), 2 findings:** fixed the cached favicon (new file names);
skipped the keyframes living in the page.

**For Dmytro on screen:**

- The browser tab shows the three orange loops (a hard refresh may be needed once).
- An unknown address, such as `/nowhere`, signed in and signed out: no sidebar, the
  logo top left, a large 404, "Page not found", the sentence, the transfer diagram
  with the dashes moving up to a red cross and a faded line to a dashed "This page",
  and "Back to Work" (signed out it leads to sign in).
- Dark and light; a narrow window (smaller 404); with reduced motion the dashes stand
  still.
